FROM MOTION TO A MATRIX

Three simple steps.
One expressive little mirror.

  1. 01

    See the shape.

    A camera measures the scene. Our virtual subjects give you a clean silhouette to experiment with, without buying any hardware.

  2. 02

    Break it into pixels.

    Each square samples part of the shape. An 8 × 8 grid has 64 lights; 16 × 16 has 256. More pixels reveal more of a pose.

  3. 03

    Let the lights follow.

    Refresh the grid as the subject moves. Holding still keeps the shape lit. A trail can make its previous positions visible too.
